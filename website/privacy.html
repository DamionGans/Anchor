Privacy Policy

Anchor Metronome ("Anchor") for Android and iPhone collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporters, or tracking of any kind.

  • Data we collect: none. Anchor does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal or usage data, and contains no third-party SDKs.
  • Settings on your device: your tempo, time signature, sounds, accents, calibration offsets, and theme are saved only on your device (Android SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults) and are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling Anchor deletes them.
  • Network access: the Google Play, App Store, and F-Droid versions make no network requests at all; updates come from the store. The free version downloaded from GitHub checks the public GitHub Releases page (api.github.com) for newer versions. That request contains no personal data; GitHub's own privacy statement applies to it.
  • Permissions: vibration (beat haptics), notifications and background playback (media controls while the screen is off or another app is open). Anchor never accesses your microphone, camera, contacts, location, or files.
  • Purchases: paid store versions are bought through Google Play or the App Store; Anchor never sees your payment details.
  • Children: Anchor collects no data from anyone, including children.
  • Contact: questions can be raised at github.com/DamionGans/Anchor/issues.
