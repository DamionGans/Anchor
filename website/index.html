Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I keep the metronome running while looking at sheet music?
Yes, absolutely. When Anchor is playing, you can switch to your sheet-music reader or PDF app, or lock your screen, and the beat will continue without missing a tick. You can Play/Pause and nudge tempo directly from your notification drawer (Android) or Lock Screen / Control Center Now Playing card (iPhone).
Why do wireless Bluetooth earbuds lag, and how does Anchor fix it?
Wireless Bluetooth headphones take 100–250 ms to buffer and decode audio, making the visual flash or vibration feel noticeably early. In Anchor, open Settings → Synchronization & Latency and adjust the Bluetooth Audio Offset slider so the visual downbeat and tactile pulse line up perfectly with what you hear in your ears.
How do setlists and the set-list bar work on stage?
Open the Playlists sheet (or press PageDown / ] on Windows), build an ordered setlist from your song library, and select it. Anchor displays an active set-list bar directly on the main screen. When you finish a tune, simply tap Next (or hit your desktop pedal/shortcut) to jump straight to the next song's tempo, time signature, and accent pattern without stopping playback.
Does Anchor work completely offline without an internet connection?
Yes, 100%. Anchor requires no internet access to run, make clicks, or access your saved library. It has zero accounts, zero analytics, and zero advertising SDKs. The Android store build removes network permissions entirely.
Is Anchor really free? Are there any ads or subscriptions?
Yes. Anchor is free and open source on GitHub with all features included. Paid Google Play and App Store releases are coming soon (along with F-Droid) as one-time purchases for users who prefer standard app stores and wish to support ongoing development. Every version is completely ad-free, subscription-free, and tracker-free.
How do I install Anchor on Windows?
1. Under Platforms above, download the MSI installer for your architecture: x64 for standard Intel/AMD PCs or arm64 for ARM/Snapdragon devices.
2. Run the downloaded .msi file. It installs directly into your user profile without needing administrator rights.
3. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info and then Run anyway. Future updates will upgrade your installation in place.
How do I install Anchor on Android?
1. Tap Get Anchor above or scan the QR code with your phone camera.
2. Open the downloaded .apk file. If prompted, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
3. Future updates can be checked and downloaded right inside the app settings with one tap.
How do I install Anchor on iPhone?
The native iPhone app requires iOS 16+. Currently you can download the unsigned .ipa from the iOS GitHub Action build and install it using your preferred sideloading tool (such as AltStore or Sideloadly), or build it in Xcode from source. An App Store listing is in preparation.
