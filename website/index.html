Everything You Need to Lock In Your Groove

Whether you are shedding rudiments on your phone, practicing at the piano with a tablet, or teaching in a studio with a laptop, Anchor stays out of your way so you can focus on playing.

6 Click Voices

Crisp, Musical Sounds

Switch effortlessly between Tick, Click, Woodblock, Cowbell, Beep, and Hi-Hat. Every sound is pre-synthesized PCM voiced to cut cleanly through acoustic instruments or in-ear monitors without harshness.

Meters & Subdivisions

Custom Accents & Grooves

Tap any beat indicator to accent it, make it a standard beat, or mute it completely. Anchor remembers your custom accent pattern for each meter count so switching time signatures never wipes your groove.

Why Musicians Switch to Anchor

Many metronomes swallow the very first click, drift out of time, or bury essential controls behind pop-up ads and subscriptions. Anchor was built from scratch to fix that on every device.

Every Beat Counts—Including Beat One

Have you ever pressed Play on a metronome app only to hear silence on the first downbeat while your phone or PC speaker wakes up? Anchor keeps the audio hardware primed so Beat 1 always rings out loud, crisp, and right on time across Android, iPhone, and Windows.

Every platform runs the same single-screen, non-scrolling interface with quick BPM steppers, multi-tap tempo, clear visual beat indicators, and zero bloat.

  • Dedicated Audio Engines: Low-latency native audio on Android (AudioTrack), iOS (AVAudioEngine), and Windows (SourceDataLine)

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install Anchor on Windows?
1. Under Platforms above, download the MSI installer for your PC architecture: x64 for standard Intel/AMD PCs, or arm64 for Snapdragon/ARM PCs.
2. Run the downloaded .msi installer. It installs per-user into your user profile and needs no administrator rights.
3. Since the MSI is currently unsigned, Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC": click More info and then Run anyway. Future updates will upgrade your installation in place.
How do I install Anchor on Android?
1. Tap Download for Android above (or scan the QR code with your phone's camera).
2. Open the downloaded .apk file. If prompted, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
3. Once installed, future updates can be checked and downloaded right inside the app's Settings menu with a single tap. Google Play and F-Droid listings are coming soon.
How do I install Anchor on iPhone?
The native iPhone app requires iOS 16+. Currently you can download the unsigned .ipa from the iOS GitHub Action build and sign it with your own developer account / sideloading tool (such as AltStore or Sideloadly), or open iosApp/ in Xcode to build it from source. A standard App Store release is currently in preparation.
What keyboard shortcuts are available on Windows?
When the Anchor window is focused on Windows, you can control the metronome without touching the mouse:
• Space: Start or stop playback (Play / Pause)
• ↑ or +: Increase tempo by 1 BPM (hold Shift for 5 BPM)
• ↓ or -: Decrease tempo by 1 BPM (hold Shift for 5 BPM)
• T: Tap tempo repeatedly to match a rhythm
Do Android, iPhone, and Windows share the same rhythm and sound?
Yes, 100%. All three platforms run the exact same shared Kotlin Multiplatform core: ClickSynthesizer for identical PCM click sounds, DelayCompensationEngine for millisecond-exact synchronization, and MetronomeSequencer for slew-limited BPM adjustments and phase calculations. The beat will never drift, and your saved settings are identical.
Is it really free? Are there any ads or tracking?
Yes. The full app is free and open source on GitHub (and F-Droid). Paid Google Play and App Store versions with identical features are coming soon for anyone who prefers one-tap store installs and wants to support development. Every version has no ads, no subscriptions, no analytics, and no account sign-ups, and works completely offline. See the privacy policy.
Can I keep the metronome running while looking at sheet music?
Absolutely. When Anchor is running, you can switch to your sheet music app, chord chart, or lock your screen and the beat will continue uninterrupted. You can Play/Pause and nudge the tempo up or down directly from the multimedia player in your notification drawer (Android) or Lock Screen / Control Center (iOS).
Why do the visuals feel slightly ahead when I use Bluetooth headphones?
Wireless Bluetooth headphones naturally take a split second to buffer audio from your device. Open Settings → Synchronization & Latency in Anchor and adjust the Bluetooth Audio Offset slider until the visual beat and flash match what you hear in your headphones.
