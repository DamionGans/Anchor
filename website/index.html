No Compromises

Why Musicians Switch to Anchor

Many mobile metronomes swallow the very first click, drift out of time, or bury basic controls behind pop-up ads and subscription paywalls. Anchor was built to fix that.

Every Beat Counts—Including Beat One

Have you ever pressed Play on a phone metronome only to hear silence on the first downbeat while your phone's speaker wakes up? Anchor silently prepares your phone's audio hardware the instant you tap Start, so Beat 1 always rings out loud, crisp, and right on time.

Everything is designed for one-handed use on a music stand: quick BPM steppers, multi-tap tempo, clear visual beat indicators, and zero scrolling.

  • Zero Ads or Paywalls: Never interrupted mid-practice by a video ad

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install Anchor on my Android phone?
1. Tap Download for Android above (or scan the QR code with your phone's camera).
2. Open the downloaded .apk file. If prompted, allow your browser to install apps.
3. Once installed, you can check for and install future updates right inside the app's Settings menu with a single tap.
Is it really free? Are there any ads or tracking?
Yes. The full app is free and open source on GitHub (and F-Droid). Paid Google Play and App Store versions with identical features are coming soon for anyone who prefers one-tap store installs and wants to support development. Every version has no ads, no subscriptions, no analytics, and no account sign-ups, and works completely offline. See the privacy policy.
Can I keep the metronome running while looking at sheet music?
Absolutely. When Anchor is running, you can switch to your sheet music app, chord chart, or lock your screen and the beat will continue uninterrupted. You can Play/Pause and nudge the tempo up or down directly from the multimedia player in your notification drawer or lock screen.
Why do the visuals feel slightly ahead when I use Bluetooth headphones?
Wireless Bluetooth headphones naturally take a split second to receive audio from your phone. Open Settings → Synchronization & Latency in Anchor and adjust the Bluetooth Audio Offset slider until the visual beat and flash match what you hear in your headphones.
